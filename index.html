Finding creators and tracking every contact

Tavo Labs had no large marketing budget, so creators were the main growth lever. I needed to find ones who fit a wellness brand, approach them, ship gifted product and follow each one through to a posted video.

My role
Researched creators on Instagram and TikTok, contacted and followed up with them, arranged shipments, and kept the tracker current.
Tools
Reacher, Kalodata, TikTok Shop affiliate tools, a tracker spreadsheet, Asana.
Result
47 creators logged, 37 contacted, 37 sent gifted product. The tracker followed each from first message to shipping, delivery, content due date, post link and follow-up.

One creator video changed the shop’s numbers

On 26 August 2026, four videos from the same campaign went live on the same day. Three behaved like tests. One broke out.

The winner reached 190K views, 796 likes, 25 comments and 103 shares. Over the 28 days to 12 September the shop had 204,827 video views and 198,332 people reached, and about 71% of its year-to-date TikTok views fell in that window. Profile views rose by 396.

My role
Sourced and briefed the creator, reviewed results in TikTok Business Suite, and wrote up what it meant for the founder.
What I took from it
It wasn’t a scripted pitch. The creator was easy to watch and the content felt authentic, so fit and delivery matter as much as follower count. Growth came in bursts tied to specific creators, so the next step is a repeatable process: test several creators cheaply, back the ones who show real signal, and write down what worked.

Replacing WhatsApp with one tracked system

Coordination across Shopify, Amazon UK/Ireland and TikTok Shop was scattered across WhatsApp. I built and ran four Asana projects covering brand strategy and goals, TikTok Shop affiliate strategy, multi-channel operations and a content calendar.
